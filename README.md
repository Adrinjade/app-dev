My Favorite Movie: Hacksaw Ridge 🎬
Introduction

My favorite movie is Hacksaw Ridge. It is a historical war movie released in 2016 and directed by Mel Gibson.

Movie Description

The movie tells the true story of Desmond Doss, a brave American soldier who served as a medic during World War II. He refused to carry or use a gun because of his religious beliefs, but he still wanted to help save lives.

Why I Love This Movie

I love this movie because it teaches important lessons about:

Courage – Being brave even when you are afraid.
Faith – Staying true to your beliefs.
Respect – Valuing every human life.
Kindness – Helping others even when it is dangerous.
Determination – Never giving up despite challenges.
My Favorite Quote
"With the world so set on tearing itself apart, it don't seem like such a bad thing to me to want to put a little bit of it back together."

My Rating

I would give this movie 10/10 because it is inspiring, emotional, and full of meaningful lessons.

Conclusion

In conclusion, Hacksaw Ridge is more than just a war movie. It shows that you do not need a weapon to be a hero. For me, Desmond Doss is a great example of courage, faith, and selflessness. This movie reminds me that saving lives is one of the greatest acts of bravery.
